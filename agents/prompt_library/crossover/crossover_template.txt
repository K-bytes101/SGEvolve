1. Review both parents' logic, metrics and evaluation results; identify complementary strengths.  
2. Focus on high-impact blocks (function/class/block) where fusion or recombination yields the most significant performance gain.
3. Produce **one** best Descendant that: combines strengths and reduces weaknesses, and keeps edits minimal.

5. Prioritize substantive improvements in algorithmic quality, efficiency, and robustness without adding unnecessary dependencies. Focus on real optimization, not superficial edits.
